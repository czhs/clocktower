# Hollowmere — town lore seeds for the interiors (owner: main session; read-only for agents)

**User request (2026-10-16):** every cottage interior must be VERY visually distinct, with its own style and AMBIANCE, and
each room should almost tell a story, hinting at the lore of the town. Hints, never exposition: no text walls, no signs
that explain things. A player who peeks into several houses should start connecting the threads.

## The town in one paragraph
Hollowmere is a village in a clearing of the Gloamwood, gathered around a clock tower that has not kept true time since
"the long night" — the night the first villager vanished. Since then the town meets on the square each day, argues on the
voting circle, and locks its doors at dusk while *something* walks the lanes. Everyone suspects someone. Everyone has a
secret. (This is Blood on the Clocktower: a demon hides among ordinary townsfolk; outsiders and minions muddy the water.)

## Shared motifs (spread them, 2-4 houses each, never all in one room)
- **11:55** — the minute the tower stopped on the long night. Stopped clocks, a circled time, a pocket watch frozen at 11:55.
- **The circled date** — the same date circled on calendars / almanacs / ledgers in several houses.
- **The red wax sigil** — a small, odd red seal (a stylised eye-in-a-clock). Hidden subtly in 3 houses only (a letter,
  a bottle stopper, a book spine). Never explained.
- **The missing gear** — the tower lost a brass gear that night; the clockmaker searches for it; it is somewhere else.
- **The grimoire** — the Storyteller's book on the dais; copies of its pages, sketches of it, rumours.
- **Lantern light** — every house owns a lantern from the Lantern-maker; one house's lantern is missing or smashed.

## Distinctness rules for builders
- Vary the ROOM ITSELF, not just props: room shape (L-shape, round tower room, loft/mezzanine, low cabin, workshop),
  fireplace position (or none), window placement, floor material, wall material, ceiling height, clutter density.
- Each interior gets its OWN lighting recipe (key light colour + temperature, night glow sources) and at least one
  cheap animated ambiance element (motes, steam, sparks, firefly specks, ripples, ticking pendulum, swinging lantern).
- Each has 2-4 story props that read at the interior camera (bigger and simpler than you think — 640x360), plus small
  details for people who look closely.
- Silhouette test: thumbnails of all 15 side by side must be told apart at 25 % size by layout + palette + light alone.
