Every sound in this folder comes from a source released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain
dedication)**: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>. Attribution is not
required, but we record it anyway. No CC-BY, no NC, nothing unverified.

- Verified on: **2026-10-14**. Each source page was fetched on hshi-u1 and its licence field read:
  the Freesound pages link only to `creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/` ("Creative
  Commons 0"), and each Kenney page and the `License.txt` inside each pack say "Creative Commons
  Zero, CC0".
- Freesound sources are the public HQ previews (`…-hq.mp3`), downloaded on 2026-10-14.
- Kenney packs were downloaded as zips on 2026-10-14. Only the `.ogg` audio was kept; the zips'
  `.txt`/`.url` files were deleted after we read the licence.
- Processing: trimmed, mixed down to mono, loudness-normalised per category, and the loops baked
  seamless. Each clip ships as `.ogg` (Opus) and `.m4a` (AAC). See `docs/audio.md`.
